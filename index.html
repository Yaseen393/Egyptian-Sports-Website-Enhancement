who we are

EDLF All Over The World

Since 1983, the Egyptian Diving and Lifesaving Federation has been training divers and diving instructors to the highest level of safety, commitment and professional work.

The federation was founded after Egypt regained Sinai, when diving and lifesaving became a big part of sport and tourism on the Red Sea. In the same year it joined the International Life Saving Federation (ILS), and soon after the World Underwater Federation (CMAS).
